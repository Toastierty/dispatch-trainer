🚁 Night shift — Comm Center (practice)

Calls arrive in real time on phone lines and radio channels. Answer, ask the right questions, request aircraft from pilots, build flight plans, launch, flight-follow, handle ER notifications and page teams. A report card with coaching appears at the end.

How it works

  • Lines ring (left). Click Answer. Ask questions with buttons or type them (“what’s your callback?”). Callers only tell you what you ask.
  • Flight requests: Request aircraft → pilot weather check (don’t share patient details) → page crew → flight plan (pilot reviews) → launch → notify requester with ETA.
  • Radio: units call status changes and position reports — acknowledge them. Watch the check-in timers on the Units tab; act fast on overdue aircraft.
  • ER notifications: EMS radios a report → take it, classify, then use the Pager tab: template, fields, recipients, preview, send.
  • Use Pause any time. Time runs 10× real time at 1× speed.
