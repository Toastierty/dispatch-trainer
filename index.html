Suggested 4-week plan before orientation

  1. Week 1: Phonetic alphabet + 24-hr time until instant. Quiz daily.
  2. Week 2: Aviation/weather terms; Map Game (hospitals first, then counties).
  3. Week 3: Dispatch Sim — each call twice; read the coaching notes.
  4. Week 4: CAD Board drills with the flight-following timer running; mixed quizzes.

Habits of a strong coordinator

  • Get a callback number first — calls drop.
  • Read back numbers: coordinates, frequencies, times, weights.
  • The pilot makes the weather go/no-go decision — commonly without patient details.
  • Timestamp everything (24-hr). If it isn't logged, it didn't happen.
  • Missed check-in? Act immediately — follow your overdue-aircraft plan.

24-hour time

Midnight = 0000, noon = 1200. After 1 PM add 12 (3:45 PM → 1545). Zulu (UTC) = Eastern + 4 h during daylight time (EDT), + 5 h during standard time (EST).

Generic scene-request intake (memory aid)

  1. Requesting agency, name, callback #
  2. Location: address/cross streets, county, lat/long
  3. Nature/mechanism, # patients, age, approx. weight
  4. LZ: location, ground contact unit & radio channel, hazards (wires, towers, trees, slope, debris)
  5. Destination preference (if any)
  6. Weather check → pilot accept/decline
  7. Give ETA, read back key items, timestamp
